Sushi Ben Privacy & Data Collection Policy

Effective Date: August 2, 2026

This Privacy & Data Collection Policy (the "Policy") explains how Big Brane Studios, Inc. ("Big Brane," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information in connection with Sushi Ben (the "Game"), the official Sushi Ben website, and related support and community services (collectively, the "Services").

1. Scope

This Policy applies to Services operated by Big Brane that link to it. It does not govern the independent practices of stores, console and virtual-reality platforms, social networks, embedded-media providers, or other third parties. Those services process information under their own privacy policies.

2. Information Processed Through the Game

Sushi Ben is a single-player game. Big Brane does not operate game servers, user accounts, multiplayer services, advertising systems, or gameplay-analytics systems for Sushi Ben. The Game does not collect or transmit gameplay history, save-game contents, device sensor data, or other gameplay data to Big Brane.

The Game interacts with platform-operated services for achievements and cloud saves. Those services are controlled by the platform on which you play, not by Big Brane.

- Platform user ID. The Game may temporarily read the user ID supplied by the platform only as needed to access the correct player's platform services, including achievements. The ID is not sent to Big Brane, copied to a Big Brane server, or retained by Big Brane.
- Platform username. The Game may temporarily retrieve the current player's username solely to display it to that same player within the Game. It is not sent to or retained by Big Brane.
- Achievements. The applicable platform administers, associates, and retains achievement records. Big Brane does not receive or store those records.
- Cloud saves. The applicable platform transfers, controls, and retains cloud-save data. Big Brane does not receive or store cloud saves.

Big Brane does not collect, store, or disclose platform user data through the Game. Platform-controlled account, achievement, cloud-save, purchase, entitlement, device, and service records remain subject to the applicable platform's terms, privacy policy, and user controls.

3. Information You Voluntarily Provide

We may receive information you choose to provide through email, support requests, surveys, bug reports, social-media interactions, website forms, or similar communications. Depending on what you submit, this may include:

- your name, email address, username, or social-media identifier;
- the platform on which you use the Game;
- the content of your message; and
- screenshots, logs, system information, save files, or other diagnostic material you choose to attach.

Please do not submit passwords, payment-card information, unnecessary sensitive information, or personal information about another person.

4. Website and Third-Party Platform Information

When you visit the Sushi Ben website, website hosting, security, form, and network providers may automatically process standard technical information such as your Internet Protocol address, browser and device type, requested page, referring page, and request date and time. Embedded video, music, community, social-media, or storefront content may cause your browser to connect directly to the applicable provider, which may process information under its own privacy policy.

Stores and platform operators independently process information needed to operate their accounts, purchases, entitlements, achievements, cloud saves, devices, and other platform services. Big Brane does not control those records. If you contact Big Brane about a purchase or platform issue, we receive only the information you choose to provide or that the platform provides in connection with that specific support matter.

Payment information is ordinarily collected directly by the applicable store or payment processor rather than by Big Brane.

5. How We Use Information

Platform user IDs and usernames are used temporarily within the Game only for the purposes described in Section 2. Big Brane does not receive or retain that information.

We use personal information separately provided to Big Brane through the website, support channels, or other voluntary communications to:

- respond to questions, support requests, bug reports, and privacy requests;
- operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Services;
- enforce our agreements and protect users, Big Brane, and the public;
- prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, and unlawful activity; and
- comply with legal obligations and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on performance of a contract, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the Services, your consent, or compliance with legal obligations, as appropriate.

6. Disclosure of Information

The disclosures in this section apply only to personal information separately provided to or controlled by Big Brane, such as website or support communications. They do not apply to platform user data temporarily accessed by the Game, which Big Brane does not receive or retain.

We may disclose information under our control:

- to vendors acting on our behalf to provide website hosting, security, communications, support, or similar business services;
- when you direct or consent to the disclosure;
- when reasonably necessary to comply with law or valid legal process, protect a person, investigate wrongdoing, secure the Services, or protect legal rights; or
- as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, subject to appropriate protections.

Big Brane does not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it to serve targeted advertising.

7. Retention

Big Brane does not collect or retain platform user IDs, usernames, achievement records, or cloud saves through Sushi Ben.

Separately, Big Brane may retain information that a person voluntarily submits through email, a website form, a support request, social media, or another communication. That information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to address the communication, provide support, maintain security and business records, comply with law, and resolve disputes. Website and security providers may retain technical records according to their own retention schedules and our service configuration. We delete or de-identify information when it is no longer reasonably needed, unless longer retention is required or permitted by law.

8. Access, Correction, and Deletion Requests

Any user, regardless of geographic location, may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information controlled by Big Brane. There is no fee for submitting a request.

To make a request, email legal@bigbranestudios.com with the subject "Sushi Ben Data Request." If your request concerns information previously submitted to Big Brane, identify the email address, username, approximate date, or support matter associated with that submission so we can locate it. We may request reasonable information to verify your identity and prevent unauthorized access or deletion.

Big Brane does not possess the platform user data temporarily accessed through the Game, so there is no such data in Big Brane's systems to delete. We will nevertheless review and respond to every request. If we identify personal information separately submitted to or otherwise controlled by Big Brane—for example, information contained in a support message—we will delete or de-identify it unless retention is reasonably necessary or legally required for security, fraud prevention, transaction records, compliance with law, or establishing or defending legal claims. If we cannot fulfill all or part of a request, we will explain why where required.

We generally respond within 30 days or within any different period required by applicable law. You may appeal a denied request by replying to our decision with "Privacy Appeal" in the subject line.

Deleting the Game or its local save data does not necessarily delete records independently maintained by a store or platform operator. Requests concerning a platform account, achievements, purchases, cloud saves, or other platform-controlled records must be made through that platform's privacy and account controls because Big Brane does not possess or control those records.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information under our control. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. International Processing

Big Brane is located in the United States. Information you voluntarily provide may be processed in the United States and in other countries where our providers operate. Those countries may have privacy laws different from the laws where you live. Where required, we use legally recognized safeguards for international transfers.

11. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and Big Brane does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy as the Services, platform features, providers, and applicable laws change. We will post the revised Policy and update the Effective Date. If a change materially affects how we use information already collected, we will provide any additional notice or choice required by applicable law.

13. Language

Translations may be provided for convenience and may contain inaccuracies. The English version available at https://www.sushiben.com/en/privacy is the controlling version to the extent permitted by applicable law.
