1. Scope

This Fan Content Policy (“Policy”) governs the creation and sharing of unofficial fan content based on Sushi Ben and related intellectual property owned or controlled by Big Brane Studios, Inc. (“Big Brane,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

We appreciate the creativity of our community and want to support fan expression within clear and reasonable guidelines. By relying on permissions granted under this Policy, you agree to comply with it.

2. Definitions

2.1. “Sushi Ben IP” means intellectual property owned or controlled by Big Brane relating to Sushi Ben, including characters, names, locations, story elements, artwork, music, audio, video, logos, and other proprietary content.

2.2. “Fan Content” means original, unofficial content created by you that is based on or incorporates Sushi Ben IP, including artwork, fan fiction, cosplay, videos, livestreams, gameplay clips, and similar creative works.

3. Eligibility and License Grant

3.1. You may rely on the permissions granted by this Policy only if you are at least 18 years old and have reached the age of legal majority where you live.

3.2. Subject to your compliance with this Policy, Big Brane grants you a limited, personal, non-exclusive, revocable, non-transferable, and non-sublicensable license to create and share Fan Content based on Sushi Ben IP.

3.3. You may grant an online platform only those rights reasonably necessary under its standard terms to host, display, distribute, or monetize Fan Content permitted by this Policy.

3.4. This Policy does not transfer any ownership rights in Sushi Ben IP.

3.5. This Policy grants permission in addition to rights you may independently have under applicable law. It does not restrict or eliminate fair use or other statutory exceptions or limitations.

3.6. This Policy applies only to intellectual property that Big Brane owns or controls and has authority to license. It does not grant rights in third-party material. You are responsible for obtaining any necessary third-party permissions. Material that appears incidentally during ordinary gameplay recordings remains subject to the rights and rules of its applicable owner and platform.

4. Permitted Uses

Subject to the restrictions in this Policy, you may:

4.1. Create and share Fan Content.

4.2. Livestream or otherwise publicly display Sushi Ben on platforms such as Twitch, YouTube, or similar services.

4.3. Upload gameplay videos, clips, recordings, screenshots, or similar content to platforms such as YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, or similar services.

4.4. Earn revenue from permitted Fan Content through:
- platform advertising or partner-program revenue;
- subscriptions, donations, channel memberships, or services such as Patreon;
- sponsored videos or streams and affiliate links;
- paid commissions for original fan artwork or similar original Fan Content; and
- sales of individually handmade, one-of-a-kind Fan Content items.

4.5. Sponsorships, affiliate relationships, and other commercial relationships must be disclosed as required by applicable law and platform rules. They must not be presented as relationships with or endorsements by Big Brane.

4.6. Commissioned or handmade Fan Content must be your original creative work and must not consist primarily of copied or extracted Sushi Ben assets.

4.7. All Fan Content must make clear that it is unofficial and is not created by, affiliated with, sponsored by, approved by, or endorsed by Big Brane.

5. Restrictions

Except as expressly permitted by Section 4, you may not:

5.1. Sell, license, or otherwise commercially exploit Fan Content or Sushi Ben IP.

5.2. Mass-produce, manufacture through third parties, use print-on-demand services for, wholesale, or commercially distribute substantially identical products using Sushi Ben IP, or maintain a recurring product line using Sushi Ben IP.

5.3. Use crowdfunding to finance the creation, manufacture, or distribution of a product or other use prohibited by this Policy. This does not prohibit memberships, donations, or creator-support services permitted by Section 4.4.

5.4. Create or distribute games, mods, applications, software, interactive experiences, or modified builds using Sushi Ben IP without Big Brane’s prior written permission.

5.5. Extract, distribute, or provide access to standalone or substantially unmodified game assets, including models, textures, artwork, music, audio, dialogue, video, cutscenes, scripts, code, or other game files. This restriction does not prohibit ordinary gameplay recordings, screenshots, commentary, reviews, or other transformative Fan Content permitted by this Policy.

5.6. Disclose, distribute, or use leaked, data-mined, confidential, or unreleased Sushi Ben material that Big Brane has not officially made public.

5.7. Represent or imply that Fan Content is official, created, approved, sponsored, endorsed by, or affiliated with Big Brane.

5.8. Use Sushi Ben IP as or as part of a business name, product or service name, domain name, application name, social-media account name, or other identifier in a manner likely to cause confusion about its source, sponsorship, or affiliation.

5.9. Use Sushi Ben IP in connection with content that is unlawful, defamatory, hateful, discriminatory, harassing, exploitative, or otherwise harmful to individuals or groups.

5.10. Use Sushi Ben IP in a way that could reasonably damage the reputation of Sushi Ben or Big Brane, including through pornographic, sexually exploitative, grossly offensive, deceptive, or misleading depictions.

5.11. Use Sushi Ben IP to promote, oppose, or express support for any political party, candidate, government, political movement, religious organization, religious doctrine, or religious message.

5.12. Use Sushi Ben IP in connection with NFTs, cryptocurrencies, blockchain-based assets, or similar technologies.

5.13. Use Sushi Ben IP to promote piracy, cheats, unauthorized access, circumvention of security measures, or other misuse of Sushi Ben or its services.

6. Required Disclaimer

6.1. Where reasonably practicable, publicly shared Fan Content must include the following disclaimer in its description, credits, accompanying text, profile, or another reasonably visible location:

“Sushi Ben is property of Big Brane Studios, Inc. This is unofficial fan content and is not created by, affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Big Brane Studios.”

6.2. If a platform or format does not reasonably permit the full disclaimer, you must still make the unofficial nature of the Fan Content clear.

7.2. Big Brane retains all rights, title, and interest in and to Sushi Ben IP.

7.3. You are responsible for your Fan Content, your compliance with applicable laws and platform rules, and obtaining permission for any third-party material you use.

Big Brane may allow its contractors and online platforms to exercise these rights only as necessary for those purposes.

7.5. The license in Section 7.4 does not permit Big Brane to sell your Fan Content as a standalone product or place it on merchandise without your separate permission. Big Brane will provide attribution where reasonably practicable.

7.6. You may ask Big Brane to stop new promotional uses of your Fan Content by contacting us. Big Brane will make reasonable efforts to honor the request, but it is not required to recall, destroy, or remove materials already produced, distributed, or published.

8. Enforcement and Termination

8.1. Big Brane may request removal or modification of Fan Content, revoke permissions granted under this Policy, disable access where available, or take legal action where appropriate.

8.2. When a violation can reasonably be corrected and does not present an urgent legal, safety, reputational, or third-party concern, Big Brane may provide a reasonable opportunity to correct it.

8.4. After receiving notice that permission has been revoked for particular Fan Content, you must stop creating or distributing the affected content and remove or modify it within the period stated in the notice.

8.5. Revocation of permission under this Policy does not affect rights that cannot lawfully be restricted, including applicable fair-use rights.

9.1. We may update this Policy from time to time. A revised Policy becomes effective when posted with a new “Last Updated” date.

9.2. Material changes apply prospectively. Where reasonably practicable, Big Brane will provide a reasonable period for existing Fan Content to be brought into compliance.
